This profile and sign-in are a simulation. No identity verification, secure authentication or role-based authorization exists, and the page guard can be bypassed.

Data handling

  • Files you choose are read in your browser and are not uploaded anywhere.
  • The current recording is held in session storage only (small CSVs) and cleared on logout or when the browser session ends.
  • Local storage holds only harmless UI preferences (and optionally a remembered sign-in email).
  • Passwords are never stored. Do not enter real patient data.

Research disclaimer

NeuroSense AI is a research and screening prototype, not a clinically validated diagnostic device. EEG alone cannot diagnose depression. Results are not a diagnosis, and clinical interpretation requires assessment by a qualified healthcare professional.
