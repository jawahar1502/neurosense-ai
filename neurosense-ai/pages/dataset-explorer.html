Label definitions

The source describes participants who scored reliably high or low on the Beck Depression Inventory (BDI). That is a self-report questionnaire grouping.

Do not assume these groups are clinically diagnosed depression. Check the dataset’s participants.tsv for the actual variables before defining labels.

Available metadata

  • Questionnaire scores — variables not verified here
  • Demographics — not verified here

License and usage notes

The source page lists a CC0 license. Even for public data, follow the dataset’s citation requests, respect participant privacy, and do not attempt re-identification. This prototype does not redistribute any dataset files.

Real analysis requires downloading the data (e.g. via OpenNeuro / NEMAR tools), decoding EEG files with a suitable library, and evaluating models with participant-independent splits. None of that is performed by this frontend.
